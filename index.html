Races are listed by fleet in start order. HW is high water; a ≈ before a time or height means it isn't printed in the programme and is estimated. "Start TBC" means the coach or instructor sets the start.

Add to calendar saves a file that opens in your phone's Calendar. The programme gives start times but not finish times, so timed entries last 3 hours unless a time range is shown, and "start TBC" items are added as all-day entries.
